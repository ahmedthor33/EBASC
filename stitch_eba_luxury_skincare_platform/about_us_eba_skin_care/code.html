Genesis of the Atelier

Engineered in Lahore and Karachi to Defy Imported Assumptions.

For decades, the Pakistani luxury market was flooded with European and North American dermatological imports. While undeniably prestigious, these dense, occlusive formulations were created for sub-zero alpine conditions or controlled continental weather. When applied beneath a 42°C sweltering sun in Karachi or during the heavy monsoons of Lahore, they suffocated pores, trapped oxidized sebum, and triggered stubborn post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation.

EBA Skin Care was founded by clinical bio-chemists and dermal physiologists operating between state-of-the-art laboratory suites in Lahore and botanical extraction centers across Punjab. Rejecting the prevalent regional epidemic of hazardous hydroquinone, toxic mercury, and steroid-laden whitening creams, EBA pioneered clean, bio-compatible micro-emulsions.

Every vial unites cold-chain stabilized bio-actives, unadulterated Damask Rose hydrosols, and multi-weight molecular humectants. Crucially, our formulas are 100% Halal-certified, zero denatured alcohol, and strictly cruelty-free.
