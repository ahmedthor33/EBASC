## Brand & Style
This design system defines an understated, high-end e-commerce experience tailored for modern South Asian luxury skincare. The visual language conveys dermatological refinement, warmth, and quiet prestige. It strikes an intentional balance between editorial elegance (inspired by vintage apothecary and high-fashion apothecaries) and precise, modern digital commerce.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Personality:** Tactile, composed, authoritative, and sensorial.
- **Target Audience:** Discerning consumers seeking high-potency, clean, and formulated skincare tailored for regional climates, demanding effortless navigation, clear ingredient storytelling, and trusted localized checkout flows (JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Cash on Delivery).
- **Emotional Response:** Reassurance, indulgence, purity, and elevated self-care.

### Design Movement: Warm Modern Editorial
The aesthetic fuses **Warm Editorial Minimalism** with **Subtle Tonal Depth**. Layouts rely on expansive warm space, rhythmic editorial typographic hierarchies, whisper-quiet micro-borders, and tactile interactions rather than synthetic flat surfaces or aggressive neon gradients.

## Typography
Typographic discipline underpins the brand's identity. Contrast is established by pairing the literary, high-fashion cadence of `Playfair Display` with the utilitarian neutrality and mathematical clarity of `Inter`.

### Brand Wordmark Spec
The brand wordmark `EBA Skin Care` uses no graphic mark or iconography. It is strictly rendered using `display-brand`:
- **Font Family:** Playfair Display
- **Weight:** 600 (SemiBold)
- **Tracking:** High positive letter spacing (`0.15em` / `letter-spacing: 0.15em`), uppercase with subtle small-cap styling for `SKIN CARE`.

### Regional Pricing Rules (PKR / Rs.)
Pricing representations must be unambiguous and immediately scannable:
- Currency denominators follow standard regional formats: `Rs. 4,850` or `PKR 4,850`.
- The numeric amount is tracked tighter (`-0.02em`) in `Inter` semi-bold to preserve tabular visual alignment during cart tallying and product comparison.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a controlled 12-column grid system bounded by generous safe margins to create an editorial catalog feel.

### Breakpoints & Geometry
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns. Gutter: `1rem` (16px), Canvas Margin: `1.25rem` (20px). Fluid single or dual-card stack.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns. Gutter: `1.25rem` (20px), Canvas Margin: `2rem` (32px).
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns. Gutter: `1.5rem` (24px), Canvas Margin: `3rem` (48px). Max content width: `1380px` centered.

### Rhythm & Padding
Negative space is treated as an active luxury material. White/cream space isolates formulations, ingredient callouts, and clinical claims. Product listings do not compress cards into dense grids; they favor expansive cards with comfortable vertical separation (`space-xl`).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this system avoids heavy, dark drop shadows. Instead, it relies on soft natural ambient diffusion, luminous borders, and tonal elevation.

### Ambient Diffusion Profiles
- **Base Level (Resting Card / Container):** Flat, isolated by subtle hairline border (`1px solid #EADECF` or `#232830` in Men's Mode) over base canvas.
- **Hovered State (Product Lift):** `0 14px 34px -10px rgba(26, 22, 21, 0.07), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(26, 22, 21, 0.03)`. Gives the impression of a card physically rising off fine rag paper.
- **Floating Controls (Quick View / Wishlist buttons):** `0 4px 16px rgba(26, 22, 21, 0.10)`.
- **Modals & Slide-out Cart Drawers:** `0 24px 60px -12px rgba(26, 22, 21, 0.22)`.
- **Men's Mode Depth Adjustment:** Shadows shift to an obsidian glow: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)` with an inner hairline highlight `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.

## Shapes
A balanced medium-rounded aesthetic (`12px` to `16px`) delivers a contemporary, ergonomic touch without appearing cartoonish. 

- **Cards & Surfaces (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px) for product listings, modal sheets, and ingredient highlights.
- **Input Fields & Secondary Boxes (`rounded`):** `0.5rem` (8px) or `0.75rem` (12px) for structured input fields, dropdowns, and review panels.
- **Action Controls & Badges (Pills):** Full radius (`9999px`) reserved specifically for category filter chips, trust badges (COD, JazzCash), quantity pill steppers, and floating wishlist trigger buttons.

## Components

### Header & Brand Bar
- **Wordmark:** Rendered via HTML/CSS using `display-brand`. Wide tracking, centered or left-aligned on mobile, accompanied by understated navigation links in `label-uppercase`.
- **Announcement Strip:** Single-tone slim banner (`#1A1615` with `#FBF7F2` text, or Champagne `#C5A880`) for store promotions (e.g., "Complimentary Nationwide Shipping on Orders Above Rs. 3,500").

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** 16px rounded surface (`#FFFFFF` in light modes, `#181B20` in dark mode) framed with a subtle 1px border. Generous image container with a 4:5 vertical aspect ratio.
- **Wishlist Heart:** Top-right floating circular pill (38px × 38px), frosted background blur (`rgba(251, 247, 242, 0.8)` or dark counterpart), smooth scale transition (`transform: scale(1.08)`) on hover.
- **Quick View Button:** Appears centered on bottom edge of image container via vertical slide and opacity fade (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
- **Ratings:** 5-star cluster rendered in champagne gold (`#D4AF37`), paired with review count formatted as `(42)`.
- **Pricing:** Prominently set in `price-md`. Original price struck through in `#8C827A` when discounted, accompanied by a small blush or gold discount tag (`-15%`).

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Warm espresso background (`#1A1615`) with ivory text (`#FBF7F2`) and 9999px pill or 12px softened corners. Subtle scale on click.
- **Secondary / Outline:** 1px border (`#C5A880`) with champagne-tinted text; fills with soft gold tint on hover.
- **Category-Specific Buttons:**
  - *Women's:* Rich plum background (`#3A2A33`) with gold-tinted hover state.
  - *Men's:* Steel slate background (`#2F4858`) or crisp white button with black text.

### Badges & Filter Chips
- Compact height (28px - 32px), pill shape (`rounded-full`), padded horizontally by `0.75rem`.
- Font styled with `label-uppercase`. Active state uses solid espresso fill; inactive state uses subtle ivory tint with hairlines.

### Local Payment & Trust Badges
- Displayed below checkout buttons and within the footer:
  - Cash on Delivery (COD)
  - JazzCash
  - EasyPaisa
  - Bank Transfer / Visa & Mastercard
- Formatted as monochromatic or duo-tone rounded badge capsules with consistent height (24px) to preserve luxury branding.

### Form Inputs
- 48px height, 8px corner radius.
- Background: `#FFFFFF` (or `#14171C` in Men's Mode) with `1px solid #EADECF`.
- Focus state: `1px solid #C5A880` with a subtle champagne glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.2)`. Label transitions seamlessly from placeholder to upper floating text.
