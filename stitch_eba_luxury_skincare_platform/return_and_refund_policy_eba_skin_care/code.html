Section 02

Eligibility Criteria for Returns

fact_check

To safeguard sanitary protocols and assure uncompromised batch lineage across our laboratories, return consignments must satisfy three fundamental standards:

75%
Formulation Volume Residual The bottle, jar, or dropper must contain at least 75% of its initial formulated contents. Fully exhausted or substantially depleted containers do not qualify for dermal guarantees.
inventory_2
Outer Packaging & Lot Number Seal The bespoke rigid paperboard packaging and intact base batch hologram/identifier must accompany the physical bottle to verify counterfeit authenticity and manufacturing batch integrity.
receipt_long
Valid Purchase Verification A confirmed Order ID (e.g. #EBA-PK-XXXXX) or the fiscal invoice included within your delivery packet.
Section 03

Damaged or Tampered Consignments

broken_image

Each EBA luxury vessel is encased in custom molded cellulose padding to withstand transit over regional transit corridors. However, in the rare instance of courier mishandling (such as fractured amber glass, leaking pipette seals, or ruptured tamper stickers):

warning

48-Hour Critical Notification Window

Please notify our concierge within 48 hours of courier delivery. Provide clear photographs of the broken outer carton, shipping waybill, and affected vessel. Upon instant inspection, an express priority replacement is dispatched without awaiting pickup.
