Section 06 Scope: Patron Sovereignty

6. Patron Rights & Data Retention

In alignment with international privacy principles and Pakistan's Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA), every EBA Skin Care patron retains complete authority over their digital identity.

Right to Complete Erasure

You may petition for the permanent destruction of your patron profile, past skin consultation logs, and recorded communications. Requests are fulfilled within 7 business days.

Instant Opt-Out (Privilege Club)

Every promotional SMS or WhatsApp bulletin contains an instant deregistration shortcode. You may likewise adjust dispatch parameters directly from your account console.

Statutory Financial Retention: While personal contact markers can be expunged, transaction invoice copies are retained for 5 tax assessment years as mandated by the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) of Pakistan for statutory audit and sales tax validation.
